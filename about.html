Our Story

With an impressive track record spanning over 1,000 successful cell tower projects since 2020, Elevated Telecom Services, LLC has built a reputation for completing projects on time and on budget. Co-owned by Thomas Weismann II and Megan Weismann, the company is a registered limited liability company legally approved to operate in multiple states. As Elevated Telecom Services continues to grow, the owners remain focused on building strategic, long-term partnerships with their customers. Drawing on hands-on experience from industry giants, Elevated Telecom Services ensures its towers meet the highest standards while optimizing operations. The company's dedicated teams specialize in assembly, reactivation, troubleshooting, and proactive maintenance to keep towers functioning properly. Backed by strong partnerships, experienced teams, and an unwavering commitment to reliability, Elevated Telecom Services has become a name customers trust in the cell tower industry. Our dedication to the craft is unmatched. We go above and beyond to deliver exceptional service, ensuring precision in every aspect of our operations. From the initial site assessment to the final installation, we prioritize quality and efficiency at every step. Our team of experienced professionals has the knowledge and expertise to handle any telecommunications project, no matter the scale or complexity..
